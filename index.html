About

Researching robots that can understand space and act in it.

My research spans the perception–reasoning–action stack of robotic manipulation: from dexterous hand systems and 3D scene representations to spatially grounded planning and closed-loop execution in cluttered environments.

Research focus

From embodiment to spatial reasoning.

I am particularly interested in systems where perception, geometric reasoning, policy learning, and physical execution must work together rather than as isolated modules.

01 / EMBODIMENT

Dexterous robotic hands

Humanoid hand mechanisms, actuation, control, and system-level evaluation for dexterous interaction.

02 / REPRESENTATION

3D spatial cognition

Persistent semantic–geometric scene representations for grounding language and geometric relations.

03 / LEARNING

Robot learning

Reinforcement and imitation learning for contact-rich manipulation and visuomotor policies.

04 / EXECUTION

Real-world manipulation

Grasp synthesis, collision-aware motion planning, active perception, and sim-to-real deployment.
